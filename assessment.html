Student pathway

Assessment

Use design evidence to develop, produce and evaluate a reusable textile lunch bag for an intended user.

What the source pack establishes

  • Investigate the need, users, existing products and textiles.
  • Develop and communicate several design ideas.
  • Record a justified final design, materials and construction plan.
  • Use tools, equipment and processes safely under teacher supervision.
  • Test and evaluate the finished product using evidence.

The web activities organise the numbered Classroom workbook sequence into a coherent folio pathway.

A dependable evidence pathway

Investigate and define

Need statement, user needs, research, material properties, sustainability and testable criteria.

Design and produce

Annotated concepts, justified final direction, samples, resources, safe workplan and progress evidence.

Test and evaluate

Criterion-matched tests, honest results, evidence-based judgements and specific improvements.
