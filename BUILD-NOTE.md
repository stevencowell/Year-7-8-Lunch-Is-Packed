# Build note — Lunch Is Packed

This Year 7/8 Technology Mandatory student site follows the Student Course Builder family standard and the Riv Burger site as its benchmark.

- 10 coherent modules from need statement to evaluation.
- 30 theory sections, 300 student knowledge checks and 30 guided written responses.
- 10 autosaving project activities aligned to the authorised Classroom workbook sequence.
- 10 downloadable eight-slide module presentations.
- Original course hero and nine project-specific teaching diagrams.
- Autosaving, printable, downloadable and restorable student folio.
- Assessment and implementation gaps marked `Teacher to confirm`.

Private student work, old dates and unverified technical specifications are excluded.
