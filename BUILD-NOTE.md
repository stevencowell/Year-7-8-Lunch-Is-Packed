# Build note — Lunch Is Packed

This Year 7/8 Technology Mandatory student site follows the Student Course Builder family standard and the Riv Burger site as its benchmark.

- 10 coherent modules from need statement to evaluation.
- 30 theory sections, 300 student knowledge checks and 30 guided written responses.
- 10 varied, autosaving project studios with 112 evidence fields and eight structured investigation tables.
- 10 downloadable eight-slide module presentations using the deeper theory and module-specific photographs.
- Original course hero, 10 module-specific teaching photographs and nine project-specific teaching diagrams.
- Autosaving, printable, downloadable and restorable student folio with live evidence counts.
- Student self-review and readiness tools that are clearly separated from formal marking.
- A 29-page editable and printable teaching program with outcome mapping, delivery ledger and sign-off controls.
- Nine purposeful module videos, each click-to-play and loaded only when selected; no repetitive or forced video in Module 9.
- Assessment and implementation gaps marked `Teacher to confirm`.

Private student work, old dates and unverified technical specifications are excluded.
The upgraded candidate remains local until publication is explicitly authorised.
