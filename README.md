# Lunch Is Packed

A student course website for the Technology Mandatory Food and Agricultural Practices unit, **Lunch Is Packed**.

The course uses the NSW Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023), the authorised Lunch Is Packed Classroom and Drive sequence, and the Riv Burger site as its family benchmark. It is designed as a ten-module learning sequence with deeper textiles theory, purposeful visuals, varied autosaving evidence studios, 300 knowledge checks, 30 guided responses, nine purposeful click-to-play videos, classroom presentations, a consolidated folio and a full teaching program.

## Student privacy

No student submissions, names or private student photographs are included. Student writing saves only in the browser on the device being used; students must follow their teacher's directions for formal submission.

## Source boundary

Current syllabus requirements are separated from school unit details. Dates, lesson timing and submission locations remain teacher-controlled where the supplied sources do not establish a current value.

See `BUILD-NOTE.md` and `SOURCE-LEDGER.md` for the full implementation boundary.
